## Description (4000 max)
Roll for the Ship, the Cap'n and the Crew, now with a sleek neon makeover.

Ship Cap'n Crew is the classic 6-5-4 dice game, ready whenever your crew is. Roll five dice and hunt for a 6 (the Ship), a 5 (the Cap'n) and a 4 (the Crew). Once you have all three, the other two dice are your cargo, and that's your score for the turn.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap ROLL to throw all five dice
• Tap any die to hold it. Held dice glow red, rolling dice glow green
• You get three rolls per turn to find your Ship, Cap'n and Crew
• Once you have all three, keep your cargo or reroll those two dice to chase a higher score
• No Ship, Cap'n and Crew after three rolls? Your turn scores zero
• Pass the phone to the next player. When everyone has had a turn, the highest cargo wins

BUILT FOR GAME NIGHT
• Live scoreboard lights up Ship, Cap'n and Crew as you find them
• Your cargo is added up for you automatically
• Big, glowing dice that are easy to read across the table
• Satisfying spin animations, dice sounds and haptics
• Hold All and Roll All for quick changes

MAKE IT YOURS
• Free Roll: no roll limit. Keep rollin' and holdin' as long as you like
• Classic Mode: longtime player? Switch back to the original marble table, chunky buttons and all
• Switch any time. Your current turn carries over

No ads, no accounts, no sign-ups. Just dice.
