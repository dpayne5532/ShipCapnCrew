Privacy policy

Your data stays yours.

Short version: Ship Cap'n Crew doesn't collect any personal information. Here's the full policy.

Last updated October 14, 2026

Who we are

Ship Cap'n Crew is made by DanCo Worldwide ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Ship Cap'n Crew iOS app and this website, dancoworldwide.com.

The app

We don't collect any data through the app. Ship Cap'n Crew has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it doesn't send your information to us or anyone else.

The app saves two settings on your device, Classic Mode and Free Roll, so it remembers your preferences. These never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It's hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard technical information such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for more.

Emailing us

If you contact support by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We don't share it or add you to any mailing list.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@dancoworldwide.com.
