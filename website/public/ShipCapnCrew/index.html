How to play

Ship, Cap'n, Crew,
then cargo.

Five dice, three rolls. Find a 6, a 5 and a 4. Whatever's left is your score.

01 · SHIP

Roll a 6. That's your Ship. Tap it to hold it.

02 · CAP'N

Next you need a 5 to captain the ship.

03 · CREW

A 4 completes your crew. Now you can sail.

04 · CARGO

The last two dice add up to your cargo. Keep it, or reroll to chase more.

3

Three rolls per turn. Hold any dice you like between rolls.

0

No crew, no cargo. Miss the 6, 5 and 4 and your turn scores zero.

↻

Pass it on. When everyone's had a turn, the biggest cargo wins.
