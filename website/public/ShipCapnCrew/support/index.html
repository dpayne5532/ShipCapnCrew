How do I play?

Roll five dice and look for a 6 (the Ship), a 5 (the Cap'n) and a 4 (the Crew). You get three rolls per turn and can hold any dice between rolls. Once you have all three, the remaining two dice add up to your cargo, which is your score. No Ship, Cap'n and Crew after three rolls means the turn scores zero. Pass the phone around, and the biggest cargo wins.

How do I hold a die?

Tap the die, or anywhere on its row. Held dice glow red and won't move when you roll. Tap again to release it. Hold All and Roll All change every die at once.

What is Free Roll?

Free Roll removes the three-roll limit so you can keep rolling and holding as long as you like. Turn it on in Settings (the gear icon at the top of the screen).

Can I get the old look back?

Yes. Open Settings and switch on Classic Mode to bring back the original marble table and buttons. You can switch back and forth any time, and your current turn carries over.

Can I change the app icon?

Yes. Open Settings and choose New or Classic under App Icon. iOS will confirm the change, and your Home Screen updates right away.

How do I start a new turn?

After your third roll, the ROLL button changes to NEW TURN. Tap it to reset the dice for the next player. In Free Roll, use Roll All to release your held dice and start fresh.

I can't hear the dice sound.

Check that your iPhone isn't in Silent mode and that the volume is up. Haptics follow your iPhone's system haptics setting.

Does the app collect my data?

No. Ship Cap'n Crew doesn't collect, store or share any personal information, and it has no ads or accounts. See our privacy policy for details.
